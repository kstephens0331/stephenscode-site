You have Sam Houston State University bringing in tens of thousands of students, faculty, and staff every fall, spring, and (to a lesser degree) summer. Those students need housing, food, entertainment, tutoring, phone repair, haircuts, and everything else a young, mobile population needs. Then you have the Texas Department of Criminal Justice, headquartered right here, employing a large, stable workforce of state employees and their families who are not going anywhere when the semester ends or a term ends. And layered on top of both of those, you have a historic downtown square that draws tourists and history buffs in to see the Sam Houston statue, visit the Sam Houston Memorial Museum, or walk through the Texas Prison Museum on their way to or from Huntsville State Park.

That is three different customer bases with three different rhythms, all shopping in the same small city. A restaurant near the SHSU campus lives and dies by the academic calendar, with a completely different summer than the rest of the year.
